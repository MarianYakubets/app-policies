Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a tip?

Simply enter your bill amount, select a tip percentage (15%, 18%, 20%, or 25%), and Tiply instantly shows you the tip amount and total.

How do I split the bill?

Tap the + button next to "People" to increase the number of people splitting the bill. Tiply will show you the per-person amount.

What are Pro features?

Tiply Pro unlocks:

  • Split bills with 3+ people
  • Custom tip percentage presets
  • Unequal splits (different amounts per person)
  • Tax calculations
  • Apple Watch app
  • Home Screen widget

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and tap "Restore Purchases". Your Pro features will be restored if you've previously purchased them.

Does Tiply work offline?

Yes! Tiply works completely offline. No internet connection required.

How do I change the currency?

Tiply automatically uses your device's currency settings. To change it, go to your device's Settings > General > Language & Region.

Is my data private?

Yes. Tiply doesn't collect any personal data. All calculations stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
