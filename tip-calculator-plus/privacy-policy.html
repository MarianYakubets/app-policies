Privacy Policy

Tiply: Tip Calculator

Last updated: December 18, 2025

Overview

Tiply: Tip Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our tip calculator application.

The short version: Tiply: Tip Calculator does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

Tiply: Tip Calculator is designed with privacy in mind:

  • No account creation required
  • No personal data collection
  • No analytics or tracking
  • No advertising
  • No third-party services that collect data

Data Storage

All app data (your preferences, settings, and calculations) is stored locally on your device using iOS's built-in storage mechanisms. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to any servers
  • Is not accessible to us or any third parties
  • Can be deleted by uninstalling the app

Third-Party Services

Tiply: Tip Calculator does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. We do not use:

  • Analytics services
  • Advertising networks
  • Crash reporting services that collect personal data
  • Social media integrations

Share Feature

Tiply: Tip Calculator includes a share feature that allows you to share calculation results. When you use this feature:

  • The sharing is handled entirely by iOS's native share sheet
  • We do not have access to how or where you share your calculations
  • No data is sent to our servers

Children's Privacy

Tiply: Tip Calculator does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 years of age. The app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Your Rights

Since we don't collect any personal data, there is no personal information for you to access, modify, or delete through us. All your app data is stored locally on your device and can be deleted by uninstalling the app.
